Privacy Policy

DEADGRID: GRIDLOCK · Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data the game DEADGRID: GRIDLOCK ("the game", "we") collects, why, how long it is kept and how to delete it. The game is published by the developer of DEADGRID (GitHub: afonsoquinaz), who is the data controller.

Short version: you can play without an account and without giving us your name or email. Online features use an anonymous account you can delete from the game at any time. We do not sell your data.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your campaign progress, settings, saved runs and an encrypted session file are stored on your device. We cannot see them unless you use an online feature that sends data (below). Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Online account and features

When you first use an online feature (leaderboards, Daily Outbreak, Sunday Siege, friends, Outposts), the game creates an anonymous account. No email, phone number or real name is asked for.

3. Gameplay statistics

The game sends aggregated gameplay statistics without your account ID: tutorial steps completed, stage win rates, the wave where runs end, card picks, economy flows, ad and store events. We use them to fix difficulty and the tutorial. Kept 13 months. Crash reports come through Apple if you allow sharing with developers.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We receive Apple's signed transaction to deliver what you bought.

5. Advertising

The game can show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch) and limited interstitial ads between waves, never during a wave. Ads are delivered by AppLovin (AppLovin MAX) and its ad network partners, which may process device identifiers, coarse location, ad interactions and diagnostics according to their own policies. Personalized ads and tracking are used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (and, where required, give consent in the consent form); otherwise ads are non-personalized. Any purchase of 2.99 or more removes interstitial ads. See AppLovin's privacy policy.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Performance of our service to you (online features, purchases), legitimate interests (anti-cheat, aggregated statistics, security), legal obligation (financial records) and consent (personalized ads).

7. Delete your data

In the game: Settings → Delete Online Account, tap again to confirm. This immediately deletes your online account, player name, friends, Outpost membership, runs, leaderboard entries and in-game wallet. Purchase records are kept only as anonymous financial records. Progress saved on the device stays until you delete the app. For any other request (access, correction, portability, objection), open a ticket asking us to contact you; do not post personal data in it. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Where the law requires consent for younger players, ads are non-personalized. If you believe a child gave us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers that run the game for us (Supabase hosting, Apple for purchases, AppLovin and its partners for ads) or when required by law. Your player name, flag and scores are visible to other players on leaderboards.

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Server access is limited by row-level security so players can read only their own private data. Scores are verified on the server before they appear on leaderboards.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the game changes what it collects, and change the date above.
