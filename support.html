Support

Help for DEADGRID: GRIDLOCK on iPhone and iPad.

Found a bug or need help? Open a ticket on GitHub with your device model, iOS version and what happened. Tickets are public: never post payment details or other personal data there.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Cancel the Survivor Club subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DEADGRID: GRIDLOCK → Cancel. You keep the benefits until the end of the paid period.

My progress is gone

Campaign progress is saved on the device. Deleting the app deletes that save. Online features (leaderboards, friends, Outposts) are tied to an anonymous online account created on first use.

Delete my online account

In the game: Settings → Delete Online Account, then tap again to confirm. This removes your online profile, friends, Outpost membership and leaderboard entries. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My score is not on the leaderboard yet

Top runs are replayed on the server to check them before they appear. This can take a few minutes. Runs with boosters or revives appear only on the Open boards.

Performance and battery

Turn on Settings → Reduce motion and lower the game speed on older devices. The game caps its frame rate outside battles to save battery.
